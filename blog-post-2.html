How to Prepare a Strong Tourist Visa File

Published on May 08, 2026 by THB VISA Expert

A tourist visa rejection is often not due to a lack of funds, but a lack of clarity and structure in the application. At THB VISA, we specialize in "File Engineering"—structuring your application so that the visa officer sees a clear, genuine, and low-risk traveler. Here is our expert guide on building a strong tourist visa file.

1. The Power of a Detailed Itinerary

Don't just say you are visiting Paris. Provide a day-by-day plan. Specify the landmarks you'll visit, the cities you'll transit through, and where you'll stay. A logical itinerary proves you've done your research and genuinely intend to travel.

2. Financial Stability Over "Big Balances"

Many applicants believe that putting a massive amount of money in their bank account right before applying will help. In reality, this is a red flag. Visa officers look for consistency. They want to see a regular source of income (salary or business profit) and steady spending patterns over at least 6 months.

3. Establishing Strong Home Ties

The most common reason for rejection is "Section 214(b)"—the belief that the applicant will not return. You must prove you have strong reasons to come back to Bangladesh. This includes:

  • Employment: NOC from your employer or business trade license.
  • Family: Marriage certificates or family relationship documents.
  • Property: Ownership documents for land or apartment (if any).

4. The Importance of the Cover Letter

Your cover letter is your voice in the application. It should briefly explain who you are, the purpose of your trip, how you will fund it, and why you will return. A professional, well-written cover letter can make a significant difference.

5. Accurate Documentation

Ensure all your documents are translated and notarized if necessary. Discrepancies in names or dates can lead to instant rejection or even bans. Double-check your passport validity—it should usually be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned stay.
