Medical Visa

Compassionate Care, Priority Processing

Health & Wellness

THB VISA understands that medical travel is often urgent and stressful. Our dedicated team provides priority support for patients seeking treatment abroad, ensuring that all medical documentation and embassy requirements are handled with extreme care and speed.

We assist in coordinating with international hospitals for invitation letters and ensure that attendants' documentation is equally robust for a smooth journey.

Our Medical Visa Support:

  • Priority Document Verification
  • Hospital Invitation Coordination
  • Attendant Visa Strategy
  • Emergency Appointment Assistance
  • Post-Treatment Extension Guidance

Required Documents

Medical Reports

Original medical reports and referral letters from local doctors/hospitals.

Hospital Invitation

Official letter from the foreign hospital detailing the treatment and costs.

Attendant Proof

Relationship proof and financial documents for the person accompanying the patient.
