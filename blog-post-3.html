New Business Visa Regulations for Schengen Area

Published on May 05, 2026 by THB VISA Expert

The Schengen Area is undergoing significant digital transformations in 2026. For Bangladeshi business travelers and entrepreneurs, understanding these new regulations is crucial to avoiding delays or rejections. THB VISA remains at the forefront of these updates to ensure our clients have a seamless travel experience.

1. The Rollout of ETIAS

While ETIAS (European Travel Information and Authorization System) primarily affects visa-exempt countries, its implementation has also led to more rigorous security checks for visa-required nationals like Bangladeshis. The digital screening process is now more integrated across all Schengen member states.

2. Updated Visa Information System (VIS)

The VIS has been upgraded in 2026 to include more biometric data and automated cross-checks against other EU security databases. This means that any previous immigration issues in any Schengen country will be instantly visible to the officer reviewing your current business visa application.

3. Stricter Proof of Business Intent

The days of generic invitation letters are over. In 2026, Schengen embassies require more detailed proof of business intent. This may include:

  • Confirmed meeting schedules or event registrations.
  • Proof of previous business transactions between the companies.
  • Evidence of the applicant's role and why their presence is physically required.

4. Financial Solvency Requirements

The minimum daily amount required for a stay in several Schengen countries (like France and Germany) has been adjusted for 2026 to reflect inflation. You must ensure your bank statements show sufficient liquidity to cover these new thresholds.

5. Processing Time Warnings

Due to the increased digital checks, "last-minute" business visa applications are becoming riskier. We recommend applying at least 45 days before your intended travel date, even though the official rule allows for a minimum of 15 days.
